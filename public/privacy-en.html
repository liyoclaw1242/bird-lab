Privacy

A little bird checked for you: we only keep what the game needs to run.

Google sign-in is optional

If you sign in, we only store the account ID that Google provides, so your birds can be tied to your account. We do not store your name, email address or profile picture.

Game data

We store the birds you own, your team, your daily draw and fight records, and records of who claimed birds from share links.

Data on your device

Your browser stores a sign-in token and any fight in progress. The server only stores a hash of the token.

IP addresses

To limit mass account creation, we temporarily keep hashes of IP addresses and delete them after one hour. Our service providers (Vercel, Supabase, Cloudflare, Google) may also keep access logs under their own terms. The page font is served by Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google when loading it.

We do not sell data, and there is no ad tracking.
