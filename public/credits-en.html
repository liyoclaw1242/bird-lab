Bird names and taxonomy

IOC World Bird List v15.2, edited by Frank Gill, David Donsker and Pamela Rasmussen, CC BY 3.0. Changes: for birds without a Traditional Chinese name, the name was machine-converted from the Simplified Chinese name.

Body size and ecological measurements

AVONET: Tobias et al. 2022, Ecology Letters, doi:10.1111/ele.13898, CC BY 4.0. Changes: attack, defense, type and rarity are derived from the measurements. They are game values, not the original data.

Fun facts on the cards

The “First impression” and “Did you know” notes come from the Chinese data in [removed], rewritten by the original author from Chinese Wikipedia and other sources, CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes: machine-converted to Traditional Chinese. The Traditional Chinese version is likewise offered under CC BY-SA 4.0. The “source” link on a card opens the main Wikipedia article it draws on. Conservation status

The category on each card is taken from Wikidata (CC0). The original assessments come from the IUCN Red List and may lag the latest assessment; the IUCN website is authoritative. The category is also one of the factors used to calculate rarity.
